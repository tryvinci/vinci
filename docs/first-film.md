# Make your first scene

A 10-minute walk from an empty canvas to a finished shot, all in free demo mode. No keys, no cost. Made for marketers, filmmakers, and creators alike: no engineering background needed.

## 1. Open your Studio

Follow the [quickstart](quickstart.md) until `./scripts/vinci studio` prints a local address, then open `http://127.0.0.1:4310/studio` in your browser.

## 2. Start from a template

Open **Explore** and pick a template close to what you want to make. Templates are finished workflow graphs: a product spot, a scene sequence, a short. Picking one fills in the steps for you.

## 3. Describe your shot

Type what you want to see in plain words. Camera language works: "35mm, golden-hour backlight, slow push-in on a coffee cup on a diner table." The Cinema Stack canvas turns one prompt into a set of connected stills: establishing shot, coverage, close-up, reverse.

## 4. Generate

Hit **Generate**. In demo mode, Vinci renders with practice providers: fast, free, and clearly marked as mock output. The job is priced before it runs, and the receipt is saved in your library.

## 5. Take it further

- Turn a still into motion with the **still-to-talk** or **video-generate** skills.
- Cut a vertical version with burned-in captions using **reframe-9x16** and **captions-burn**.
- Find anything you made later with `vinci find`.

## 6. Go live when you are ready

Open **Connect** in the Studio and add a provider key (Wavespeed, Google, fal, ElevenLabs, Cloudflare, or sign in to Higgsfield or Magnific). Real spend stays off until you enable it, and every live job still shows its price before it runs.
